# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Huiguang" (回光) iOS application (Bundle ID: `com.yuanjingtech.xiuxian`, the "App"), provided by Yuanjing Tech ("we", "us").

## 1. In One Sentence

**Huiguang keeps your data on your phone.** The App has no accounts, makes no network requests, and does not collect, upload, or share any of your personal data. Everything you record stays in the App's sandbox on your own device and is visible only to you.

## 2. Information We Process

The following information is entered by you or generated by your practice, and is stored only on your device:

| Category | Details | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Practitioner name | The name you enter in "Settings" (default "修习者") | A display-only label in the UI; not an identity identifier |
| Practice progress | Total meditation minutes, accumulated Qi, current realm, session count, streak days | Calculate chapter and realm unlock status |
| Session records | Date, course name, chapter name, duration, and Qi for each session | Generate the session log, seven-day chart, and statistics |
| Daily check-ins | Daily task completion per date | Show today's progress, calendar marks, and streaks |
| Practice notes | Text you write in "Practice Notes" | Shown back to you only, for neutral self-observation |
| Today's plan | The meditation plan you choose | Display today's schedule |
| Preferences | Voice on/off, speech rate, daily reminder on/off and time | Present voice guidance and reminders according to your preferences |

**None of this information is sent to any server.** The App contains no networking code and integrates no third-party SDKs (no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no push provider). It does not use iCloud or CloudKit sync.

> We do **not** collect: your name, phone number, email, government ID, location, contacts, photos, clipboard, device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), or Health app data. We do not build user profiles and do not track you across apps.

**About "realms" and "Qi"**: realms, Qi, breakthroughs, and tribulations in the App are **fictional, game-like elements** driven solely by the local numbers above. They do not represent any actual spiritual attainment, religious realization, or real-world effect, and do not constitute any measurement or assessment of your health or mental state.

## 3. Where Data Is Stored

1. **On-device app sandbox.** The data above is JSON-serialized and stored in iOS `UserDefaults` (key `xiuxian.state.v1`) inside the App's private sandbox. Other apps cannot read it.
2. **Not additionally encrypted, but protected by the OS.** The data is serialized rather than encrypted; its security relies on iOS sandbox isolation and device passcode encryption. We therefore recommend enabling a device passcode and Face ID / Touch ID.
3. **System backups.** If you enable iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone/iPad to a computer, App data may be included in that backup. That backup is managed by Apple (or by you) and never passes through our servers. You can disable backup under iOS "Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups", or exclude the App with desktop backup tools.
4. **No migration between devices.** Because there is no cloud sync, after switching to a new device you need to set up again; past session records and notes are not carried over.

## 4. System Permissions

The App requests exactly one permission:

- **Notifications** — used for the daily meditation reminder at the time you set. All reminders are **generated and scheduled locally on your device** (a repeating calendar-based local notification) and never pass through a push server. The notification text is a fixed in-app message and contains none of your personal records. You can turn it off anytime in the App ("Settings → Daily Local Reminder") or in iOS "Settings → Notifications → Huiguang". Declining does not affect other features; you simply will not receive reminders.

The App does **not** request camera, microphone, photo library, location, contacts, calendar, Health data, or any other permission.

## 5. Voice Guidance

Voice guidance is synthesized **on device in real time** using iOS's built-in speech synthesis framework (`AVSpeechSynthesizer`), with the system Chinese (zh-CN) voice. The spoken text is fixed guidance content bundled in the App and **contains none of your personal records**; synthesis makes no network request, and no audio is recorded or uploaded. You can disable voice or adjust its rate in "Settings"; once disabled, the App does not use speech at all.

> Some system voice packs may be downloaded by iOS itself from Apple on demand. That is part of Apple's system update mechanism and does not involve the App transmitting any of your information.

## 6. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

- **We do not sell** any of your information.
- **We do not share** your information with third parties — we neither collect nor hold it.
- **We do not transfer** data in connection with any merger or acquisition; the data never leaves your device.
- **We do not disclose** data publicly; we hold no copy to disclose.

The App contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and does not use your data for personalized recommendations or user profiling.

## 7. Your Rights

Because the data lives only on your device, you can exercise every right directly and completely, without contacting us:

| Right | How |
|---|---|
| Access / export | View it in the App's "Today", "Daily", and "Realm" tabs; take screenshots to take it with you |
| Rectify | Change your name, voice, and reminder preferences under "Settings"; unchecking a daily task in "Daily" also reverses its Qi for that day |
| Delete a single record | Clear the practice note and save; uncheck the corresponding task |
| Delete all data | Use "Settings → Reset All Practice Data", or uninstall the App (iOS removes everything in its sandbox) |
| Withdraw notification consent | Turn off reminders in the App, or in iOS "Settings → Notifications → Huiguang" |

Deletion is irreversible, and we hold no backup to restore it — that is both the cost and the guarantee of "your data stays with you".

## 8. Practice Notes

Text you write in "Practice Notes" may contain physical or mental sensations you chose to record. Our principle is: **collect nothing, upload nothing, retain nothing on any server** — all processing happens locally on your device and the text is shown only to you. Please also safeguard your device and passcode so others cannot view this content on your phone.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 14 and is not offered to them. Because the App collects no information and has no accounts or servers, there is no scenario in which a child's personal information is collected or disclosed. If you are under 14, please use the App only under the guidance of a guardian, or do not use it.

## 10. Data Security

Our approach is to reduce risk at the source: no networking, no accounts, no third-party SDKs, no unnecessary permissions, and data that never leaves the device. This eliminates server breaches, interception in transit, and resale by third parties. Device-side risks such as loss, jailbreaking, or shared use are mitigated by your system passcode and biometrics.

## 11. Medical Disclaimer

The App is a traditional-culture-themed meditation practice tool and a gamified habit tracker. It **is not religious ritual instruction and is not a medical device**. It makes no claim to diagnose, treat, relieve, or prevent any condition and does not replace professional medical or psychological advice. Guidance covers only sitting comfortably, relaxing, natural breathing, and attention settling, with no promised outcomes; realms and tribulations are purely fictional, game-like presentations.

Always **breathe naturally and do not hold your breath**; **stop immediately** if you feel unwell. If you have physical or mental health concerns, consult a qualified professional before practicing.

The App does not offer fortune-telling, predict personal fortunes, or promote superstition.

If a future version adds networking, cloud sync, accounts, subscriptions, or third-party SDKs, we will update this Policy before that feature ships, clearly stating the new data types, purposes, and recipients, and will highlight the change in the App. Continued use means you accept the updated Policy; if you do not, you may stop using and uninstall the App.

Previous versions and revision dates of this Policy are retained on this page.
