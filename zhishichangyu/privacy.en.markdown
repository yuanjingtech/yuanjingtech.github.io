# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Knowledge Universe" (我的知识宇宙) iOS application (Bundle ID: `com.yuanjingtech.knowledge`, the "App"), provided by Yuanjing Tech ("we", "us").

## 1. In One Sentence

**The App keeps your data on your phone.** The App has no accounts, uploads nothing, and does not collect or share any of your personal data. Everything you record stays in the App's sandbox on your own device and is visible only to you.

## 2. Information We Process

The following information is generated as you use the App and is stored only on your device:

| Category | Details | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Check-in records | Daily completion status, study minutes, consecutive-day streak, cumulative study time | Show today's progress, calendar marks, and study statistics |
| Today's study plan | The 3 knowledge tasks scheduled for the day, their status and planned duration | Build and maintain the "2 hours a day" study package |
| Carry-over queue | Tasks not finished before midnight, with their original date and number of deferrals | Move yesterday's unfinished tasks to today so nothing is lost |
| Learned and favorites | Codes of items you marked as learned or favorited | Exclude learned items and show your favorites |
| Preferences | Preferred domains (multi-select from 16 top-level domains), whether to exclude learned items, whether to only draw items with free links | Decide the blind-box draw range |
| Blind-box history | The code of the most recently drawn item | Avoid drawing the same item twice in a row |

**None of this information is sent to any server.** The App contains no networking code and integrates no third-party SDKs (no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no push provider). It does not use iCloud or CloudKit sync.

> **On data export:** Because all data stays on your own device and is never transmitted to any server (domestic or foreign), the App **does not export personal information** and is not subject to a data export security assessment, certification, or standard contract filing.

> We do **not** collect: your name, phone number, email, government ID, location, contacts, photos, clipboard, device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), Health app data, or message content. We do not build user profiles and do not track you across apps.

## 3. Where Data Is Stored

1. **On-device app sandbox.** The data above is JSON-serialized and stored in iOS `UserDefaults` inside the App's private sandbox. Other apps cannot read it.
2. **Not additionally encrypted, but protected by the OS.** The data is serialized rather than encrypted; its security relies on iOS sandbox isolation and device passcode encryption. We therefore recommend enabling a device passcode and Face ID / Touch ID.
3. **System backups.** If you enable iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone/iPad to a computer, App data may be included in that backup. That backup is managed by Apple (or by you) and never passes through our servers. You can disable backup under iOS "Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups", or exclude the App with desktop backup tools.
4. **No migration between devices.** Because there is no cloud sync, after switching to a new device you need to set your preferences again; past check-ins are not carried over.
5. **Uninstalling deletes everything.** When you delete the App, iOS removes all of its data from the sandbox, including settings and records.

## 4. System Permissions

**The App requests no system permissions at all.** It requires no notification, camera, microphone, photo library, location, contacts, calendar, health data, clipboard, or tracking access.

The App contains no push notifications, reminders, photo capture, audio recording, location, or health-data features. Every feature works fully offline.

## 5. Third-Party Links

The App ships with 673 knowledge entries, each accompanied by 3–5 links to free public resources (such as Wikipedia, Internet Archive, and various open-access archives). When you tap a link, **iOS hands the URL to the system default browser (for example, Safari)**:

- The App only performs the redirection and **does not pass along any of your records, notes, check-ins, or preferences**;
- These links point to **third-party websites**, whose privacy policies, data handling, and content are their own responsibility, not ours;
- Search-type links containing a `{q}` placeholder are automatically completed with the **title** of the knowledge entry as the search term. The title is the public name of a knowledge entry, not personal data;
- Tapping an external link takes you out of the App, and that third party may collect browsing information (such as your IP address and cookies) under its own policy. If personal information is involved, please also read that third party's privacy policy.

## 6. Knowledge Content and Sources

The knowledge data bundled in the App (entries, introductions, keywords, representative works, free links, and derived study suggestions) is **static content distributed together with the App** and stored in the app bundle:

- This content **contains no user data** and does not change based on how you use the App;
- The free-access labels shown on links (such as "free full text / open access / partially free / free registration required / free auditing") reflect the **state of the linked resource at the time**, and may change over time;
- Some links point to resources requiring free registration or payment. The App notes this in the link name and description, but the resource provider's current rules always take precedence.

## 7. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

- **We do not sell** any of your information.
- **We do not share** your information with third parties — we neither collect nor hold it.
- **We do not transfer** data in connection with any merger or acquisition; the data never leaves your device.
- **We do not disclose** data publicly; we hold no copy to disclose.

> If a future version introduces third-party analytics, advertising, or push services, we will **update this Policy before that feature ships**, clearly stating the new data types, purposes, and recipients, and will highlight the change in the App.

## 8. Your Rights

Because the data lives only on your device, you can exercise every right directly and completely, without contacting us:

| Right | How |
|---|---|
| Access / export | View it under "Check-ins", "Overview", and "Library" in the App; take screenshots or use the system share sheet |
| Rectify check-ins and minutes | Uncheck or check the corresponding task under "Today"; correct the duration with `+15 / −15 minutes` |
| Delete a single record | Uncheck that task under "Today"; remove the "Favorite" mark |
| Delete all records | Uninstall the App; iOS removes everything in its sandbox, including settings |
| Change preferences | Reset your preferred domains and filters under "Blind Box" |

Deletion is irreversible, and we hold no backup to restore it — that is both the cost and the guarantee of "your data stays with you".

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 14 and is not offered to them. Because the App collects no information and has no accounts or servers, there is no scenario in which a child's personal information is collected or disclosed. If you are under 14, please use the App only under the guidance of a guardian, or do not use it.

## 10. Data Security

Our approach is to reduce risk at the source: no networking, no accounts, no third-party SDKs, no system permissions, and data that never leaves the device. This eliminates server breaches, interception in transit, and resale by third parties. Device-side risks such as loss, jailbreaking, or shared use are mitigated by your system passcode and biometrics.

## 11. Content Disclaimer

- The knowledge introductions and study suggestions in the App are **general education and learning support**, for reference only. They do not constitute diagnosis, treatment, or any form of professional advice.
- The subject classification follows the *Catalog of Graduate Education Disciplines (2022)* as its backbone; codes serve as index only, and **the official latest version prevails**.
- Folk categories such as "the three teachings and nine schools" / "upper, middle, and lower nine schools" / "the three hundred and sixty trades" conflict across historical versions. The App adopts the more widely circulated reading and marks it "versions differ". A taxonomy is an organizing tool, not the boundary of knowledge — this table is a map, not a territory.
- Free-access terms for external resources may change over time. Parts that require registration or payment are marked in the link descriptions.
