# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-12

## Introduction

We take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information.

## Information Collection

### Local Device Storage
1. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your data to our servers.
2. Synchronization uses Apple iCloud, and data is transmitted directly to the iCloud servers.

### Automatically Collected Information
- Device model and operating system version
- Application crash logs
- Usage statistics (anonymized)

## Use of Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our services
- Diagnose and fix technical issues
- Deliver personalized experiences with your consent

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except:
1. With your explicit consent
2. When required by law or regulation
3. To protect the rights, property, or safety of us or others

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:
- Local data encryption
- Secure network transmission (HTTPS)
- Periodic security audits

## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
